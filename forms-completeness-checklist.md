## A. Patient identification & demographics

- [ ] Patient name spelled correctly on all forms
- [ ] Date of birth matches wristband / records
- [ ] MRN matches wristband and system record
- [ ] Address and phone number current
- [ ] Emergency contact listed with relationship and phone
- [ ] Preferred language documented
- [ ] Interpreter need identified and arranged (if applicable)

**Notes:** ______________________________________________________________

## B. Consents & authorizations

- [ ] General consent to treat signed and dated
- [ ] Consent signature matches patient or legal representative
- [ ] Representative relationship documented (if applicable)
- [ ] HIPAA / privacy practices acknowledgment signed
- [ ] Financial responsibility form signed
- [ ] Photo / media release signed or declined (if applicable)
- [ ] Advance directives discussed; copy obtained (if applicable)

**Notes:** ______________________________________________________________

## C. Insurance & billing

- [ ] Insurance card copied front and back
- [ ] Eligibility verified and documented
- [ ] Required authorization / referral obtained
- [ ] Copay collected and receipted
- [ ] Self-pay / uninsured options discussed (if applicable)
- [ ] Patient billing questions addressed

**Notes:** ______________________________________________________________

## D. Clinical documentation

- [ ] History and physical documented
- [ ] Medication reconciliation completed
- [ ] Allergies documented (drug, food, environmental; reaction noted)
- [ ] Immunization record obtained or requested
- [ ] Code status documented
- [ ] Fall risk / skin assessment completed

**Notes:** ______________________________________________________________

## E. Discharge & follow-up

- [ ] Discharge instructions given and reviewed
- [ ] Follow-up appointment scheduled
- [ ] Updated medication list provided
- [ ] Referral appointments scheduled
- [ ] Home-health / equipment needs arranged
- [ ] Patient / caregiver teach-back completed

1. Purpose

Why does this policy exist? What problem does it solve? Who does it protect?

2. Scope

Who and what does this policy apply to? (Staff roles, departments, settings, patient types, equipment, etc.)

3. Policy statement

The core rule or requirement, stated plainly. Use short sentences. Avoid jargon.

4. Definitions

Define any terms that could be unclear.

TermDefinition

5. Procedure

Step-by-step how to follow this policy. Number the steps.

6. Responsibilities

Who is responsible for what under this policy.

7. Compliance & monitoring

How will you know the policy is being followed? Who checks, how often, and what happens if it is not followed?
